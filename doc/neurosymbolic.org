* Symbolic layer: exact arithmetic over Q(√5)

The symbolic layer of the neurosymbolic stack. Exact field arithmetic
in Clojure (~src/qsqrt5/core.clj~), Galois conjugation in
~src/qsqrt5/galois.clj~, and the neural↔symbolic bridge in
~src/qsqrt5/bridge.clj~. All 18 clojure.test suites green (4375 assertions).

#+end_src

* Bridge contract

- Neural proposes (doubles). ~qsqrt5.bridge/snap~ recognizes an exact
  a+b√5 with small coefficients or refuses (nil). No silent coercion.
- Symbolic disposes (exact). ~qsqrt5.bridge/verify~ certifies a neural
  float against the exact value within tolerance.
- ~to-double~ is the single inexact function in the symbolic layer,
  quarantined in ~qsqrt5.core~ so exactness elsewhere is auditable.

* Sovereign compute role

This is the *symbolic layer*: exact math the neural layer can be
checked against. It does not train, does not approximate, and does
not depend on any external service.
